论文动机 / Motivation（中英对照）

统一论点：在面向 LLM 多智能体系统（以文档处理型 agent-network 为测试床）的可观测性数据上，我们系统性地采集了指标(metrics)、调用链(trace)、日志(log)三个维度，覆盖 1/5/10 并发用户、以及单副本与多副本两种部署。分析表明，下述两个动机在单副本与多副本部署下均稳定存在，且不随并发规模消失——它们是系统可观测性的内在困境，而非某次部署或采样的偶然现象。

Unifying claim: On observability data collected from an LLM-based multi-agent system (using the document-processing “agent-network” as a testbed), we gather three modalities—metrics, traces, and logs—across 1/5/10 concurrent users and under both single- and multi-replica deployments. Our analysis shows that the two motivations below *persist under both single- and multi-replica deployments** and do not vanish with concurrency. They are intrinsic observability difficulties of the system rather than artifacts of a particular deployment or sampling.*

动机一：指标的时间滞后与失真使异常时间窗口难以确定

Motivation 1: Temporal lag and distortion of metrics make the anomaly time window hard to determine

在多智能体系统中，一次请求由入口（planner）触发，经由中间工具智能体逐级调度，最终在出口（summarizer）收敛。资源与时延指标对负载的响应并非即时：以请求速率(QPS)为驱动信号做互相关分析，CPU 的峰值响应滞后约 5–35s，且内存的最佳滞后与 CPU 明显不一致（在多副本 5 用户场景中内存滞后可达 +60s），呈现出缓慢累积、长时间不回落的特征。由于不同指标的响应滞后彼此不一致，不存在单一的时间偏移能够同时对齐所有信号。

In a multi-agent system, a request is triggered at the entry (planner), dispatched level by level through intermediate tool agents, and finally converges at the exit (summarizer). Resource and latency metrics do not respond to load instantaneously: using the request rate (QPS) as the driving signal, cross-correlation shows the CPU response peaks with a lag of about 5–35 s, while memory’s optimal lag differs markedly from CPU’s (reaching +60 s in the 5-user multi-replica case), exhibiting slow accumulation that does not quickly subside. Because different metrics lag inconsistently, no single temporal offset can align all signals simultaneously.

更关键的是，这种滞后会沿调用链累积。以入口的请求为基准，入口自身滞后≈0，而下游各阶段（路由、解析、生成、出口）的响应滞后立即跳升到约 20–50s；从真实调用链的逐节点耗时看，一次逻辑请求的完成时间随链路深度单调累积，且其跨请求的离散度随深度急剧变宽（在深层阶段可从数十秒扩展到数百秒）。同时，调用链本身高度动态：链路长度从 0 到 9 层不等，单一系统内出现多达数十条互不相同的执行路径（路径熵约 3.3–4.0 bit）。这意味着”异常发生的时间窗口”对不同请求而言并不一致，难以用固定或等宽滑动窗口刻画。

More importantly, this lag *accumulates along the call chain*. Taking the entry request as the reference, the entry itself lags ≈0, whereas downstream stages (routing, parsing, generation, exit) jump to a response lag of roughly 20–50 s. From the per-node durations of real call chains, the completion time of one logical request accumulates monotonically with chain depth, and its cross-request dispersion widens sharply with depth (from tens to hundreds of seconds at deeper stages). Meanwhile, the call chain is highly dynamic: chain length ranges from 0 to 9 levels, and a single system exhibits dozens of distinct execution paths (path entropy ≈ 3.3–4.0 bits). Consequently, the “time window in which an anomaly occurs” is not consistent across requests and cannot be captured by a fixed or equal-width sliding window.

即便退一步只依赖聚合时延指标，其时间保真度同样不足。服务级 latency 指标经直方图分桶后仅塌缩到每服务 2–8 个离散取值，且 call.csv 与 latency.csv 在全部列上逐值相同（后者仅是”入口调用”视角，不含额外信息）；与真实完成时间相比，指标的 p99 时延倍率在 0.2×（低估）到 122×（高估）之间无规律地跳变。因此，聚合指标既无法刻画真实完成时间的连续分布，也无法据以稳定推断异常的起止时刻。

Even if one falls back to aggregated latency metrics alone, their temporal fidelity is insufficient. After histogram bucketing, the service-level latency metric collapses to only 2–8 discrete values per service, and call.csv is value-for-value identical to latency.csv across all columns (the former merely being the “entry-call” view, carrying no extra information). Compared with the real completion time, the metric’s p99 latency ratio jumps erratically between 0.2× (under-estimate) and 122× (over-estimate). Hence, aggregated metrics can neither describe the continuous distribution of true completion times nor support a stable inference of an anomaly’s start and end.

这一动机在单副本与多副本部署下均成立。跨 1/5/10 用户及两种副本配置，CPU 对负载的滞后一致为正、且相关强度随负载/副本增强（多副本 5 用户下 r 高达 0.94）。值得强调的是，我们唯一观察到的”负滞后”（单副本 1 用户下 −45s）源于低负载稀疏数据的噪声：其互相关曲线近乎水平、峰值不显著，重新采集后即转为物理合理的 +5s。这恰恰反证了在稀疏观测下，连”滞后/窗口”本身的估计都是不稳定的。

This motivation holds under both single- and multi-replica deployments. Across 1/5/10 users and both replica configurations, the CPU lag relative to load is consistently positive and its correlation strengthens with load/replicas (r up to 0.94 for 5-user multi-replica). Notably, the only “negative lag” we observed (−45 s under 1-user single-replica) stemmed from noise in low-load, sparse data: its cross-correlation curve was nearly flat with no significant peak, and it turned into a physically reasonable +5 s upon re-collection. This, in turn, demonstrates that under sparse observation even the estimate of “lag/window” itself is unstable.

小结与启示：面向多智能体系统的异常分析，固定或等宽的时间窗口会系统性地错位；需要链路感知、随请求自适应的时间建模，并以真实调用链的时序作为时间对齐的锚点，而非直接采用被滞后与分桶扭曲的聚合指标。

Takeaway: For anomaly analysis in multi-agent systems, fixed or equal-width time windows are systematically misaligned; what is needed is *chain-aware, per-request adaptive** temporal modeling that anchors temporal alignment on the real call-chain timing, rather than directly using aggregated metrics distorted by lag and bucketing.*

动机二：指标的稀疏性与极大方差使故障特征难以识别

Motivation 2: Sparsity and extreme variance of metrics make fault features hard to identify

多智能体系统的服务级指标极度稀疏。即使在 10 用户负载下，服务时延(latency)约 77% 的单元格为空、请求速率(QPS)约 77% 为零，真正携带信息的单元格不足四分之一；trace 维度在多次采集中甚至整体为空。从真实调用链看，稀疏性同样显著：入口 planner 出现在约 86% 的请求中，而其余工具智能体的出现率普遍低于 14%——大多数服务只在少量请求里被触发。故障若发生在这些低频服务上，其信号极易被大面积的缺失所淹没。

Service-level metrics in the multi-agent system are extremely sparse. Even under a 10-user load, about 77% of service latency cells are empty and about 77% of QPS values are zero, so fewer than a quarter of cells carry information; the trace modality was even entirely empty across several collections. From the real call chains, the sparsity is equally pronounced: the entry planner appears in about 86% of requests, whereas all other tool agents appear in under 14%—most services are exercised in only a few requests. If a fault occurs on these low-frequency services, its signal is easily buried by widespread missingness.

在指标确有取值之处，其正常态方差同样巨大。以变异系数(CV=σ/μ)度量，网络接收/时延类指标的 CV 可达 1–4；在剔除仅路由、耗时≈0 的组入口 span、只统计实际执行 span 后，计算/IO 密集型智能体的执行时间 CV 依然很大（OCR≈2.5、planner≈1.45，即标准差已超过均值）。也就是说，主导端到端时延的关键服务，其正常运行时的波动本身就极大。在如此高的基线方差下，基于均值±kσ 的阈值要么过松而漏报，要么频繁误报，故障引起的特征变化难以与固有噪声区分。

Where metrics do take values, their normal-state variance is likewise enormous. Measured by the coefficient of variation (CV = σ/μ), network-receive and latency metrics reach CV values of 1–4; after excluding routing-only group-entry spans with ≈0 duration and counting only actual-execution spans, the execution-time CV of compute/IO-intensive agents remains large (OCR ≈ 2.5, planner ≈ 1.45, i.e., the standard deviation already exceeds the mean). In other words, the key services that dominate end-to-end latency fluctuate greatly even in normal operation. Under such a high baseline variance, mean ± kσ thresholds are either too loose (missed detections) or trigger frequent false alarms, and fault-induced feature changes cannot be distinguished from intrinsic noise.

聚合指标的表达能力还进一步受限于量化与冗余。服务级 latency 经直方图分桶后每服务仅剩 2–8 个离散取值，无法刻画真实执行时间的连续分布；call.csv 与 latency.csv 完全一致，并未提供互补信息。因此，仅凭现有聚合指标构造的特征既稀疏、又高噪、还被量化压缩，判别力薄弱。

The expressiveness of aggregated metrics is further limited by quantization and redundancy. After histogram bucketing, the service-level latency retains only 2–8 discrete values per service and cannot capture the continuous distribution of true execution times; call.csv is identical to latency.csv and provides no complementary information. Consequently, features built solely from existing aggregated metrics are simultaneously sparse, noisy, and quantization-compressed, and thus weak in discriminative power.

这一动机同样在单副本与多副本部署下均成立。由于服务级指标是跨副本聚合得到的，为部分服务增加副本并不会降低其稀疏性或方差：跨 1/5 用户的单/多副本对照中，时延有效占比、QPS 非零率、以及关键服务的执行时间 CV 均保持在同一量级。副本数改变的只是集群总资源（pod 由 13 增至 18、总 vCPU/内存相应上升），而非指标的信息密度。此外，各服务的指标可观测性在不同采集之间大幅波动（由任务组合差异导致、与副本无关），进一步说明单纯依赖指标的可观测性本身就不稳定、不可靠。

This motivation also holds under both single- and multi-replica deployments. Because service-level metrics are obtained by *cross-replica aggregation*, adding replicas to some services does not reduce their sparsity or variance: in single-vs-multi comparisons across 1/5 users, the informative fraction of latency, the non-zero rate of QPS, and the execution-time CV of key services all remain at the same order of magnitude. Changing the replica count alters only the cluster’s total resources (pods increase from 13 to 18, with corresponding rises in total vCPU/memory), not the information density of the metrics. Moreover, the observability of per-service metrics fluctuates greatly between collections (driven by differing task mixes, independent of replicas), further indicating that metric-only observability is itself unstable and unreliable.

小结与启示：仅依赖聚合指标的阈值化或统计判别，在稀疏性与极大方差下难以稳定识别故障特征；需要引入多模态、以调用链为基础且对稀疏与高方差鲁棒的特征表示，将真实执行时序与结构信息纳入判别，而非停留在被量化、冗余的原始指标之上。

Takeaway: Threshold-based or statistical discrimination relying solely on aggregated metrics cannot stably identify fault features under sparsity and extreme variance; what is needed is a *multi-modal, call-chain-grounded** feature representation that is robust to sparsity and high variance, incorporating real execution timing and structural information rather than remaining at the level of quantized, redundant raw metrics.*
